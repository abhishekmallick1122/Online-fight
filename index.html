NEON FISTS

Two fighters. One neon rooftop. Win two rounds to take the title.

P1: A/D move, W jump, S or hold back to block, J punch, K hook, L kick, I spin, U special blast (uses 25 meter), O super (full meter).
P2: arrows to move/jump/block, keys 1-6 to attack. In 1 Player mode the arrows and 1-4 also work. Variety: the stage changes every round, rounds 2+ get a random twist (low gravity, double damage, speed rush, meter feast), and H / M / R orbs drop mid-fight (health, meter, power-up) in offline modes. New moves: tap block right before a hit = PARRY (attacker gets stunned). Hit them mid-startup = COUNTER. Hook (K) is an uppercut launcher: follow up with kick/spin to juggle. Kick/spin in the air = dive kick. Double-tap a direction to dash.

Keyboard: A D W S + J K L I   F: full screen
